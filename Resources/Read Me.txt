NotizEditor {VERSION}

1. Drag "NotizEditor" into "Applications".
2. Open NotizEditor from your Applications folder.
[unsigned]   The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because it isn't notarized by Apple yet.
[unsigned]   Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
3. Your notes are normal text files in Documents → Notes – press ⌘N for the first one.
